# Nuvio international collection artwork

These landscape cards were generated deterministically for Martin Chartrand's
Nuvio collection profile. The artwork uses an original composition: dark field,
luminous territorial outline, centered flag, and integrated collection label.

## Geographic outlines

- Natural Earth `ne_10m_admin_0_countries` and
  `ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces` GeoJSON.
- Source: <https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector>
- Natural Earth data is in the public domain.

## Flags

- Country SVGs: `flag-icons`, MIT license.
- Source: <https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons>
- Québec flag: Government of Québec design, public-domain SVG from Wikimedia
  Commons: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Quebec.svg>

## Generated set

- International Series: China, France, Germany, India, Italy, Japan, South
  Korea, Iran/Persia, Russia, Spain, and Québec.
- International Cinema: Québec.
- Each card has a base and focus variant at 1920×1080.
